CampusPulse System Architecture Overview

Designed to handle 5,000 students, 200 faculty members, and real-time attendance compliance across multiple academic departments.

User Persona & Permission Workflow

1. Faculty / Teacher - Daily class timetable slot selection
- Quick roll call grid & barcode scanner
- Submits correction requests with proof
2. Department Head (HOD) - Department attendance health summary
- Reviews & approves/rejects corrections
- Manages departmental defaulter list
3. Admin / Dean - Institutional compliance analytics
- Campus-wide defaulter hall ticket blockage
- Immutable audit log monitoring
4. Student - Subject-wise attendance breakdown
- "Classes Needed for 75%" calculator
- Submits excuse notes & medical certificates

Database Normalization Strategy

Entities strictly decoupled across User, Department, CourseSection, ClassSchedule, AttendanceSession, and AttendanceRecord. Indexed on (session_id, student_id) to support rapid aggregation of 360,000 monthly attendance logs.
